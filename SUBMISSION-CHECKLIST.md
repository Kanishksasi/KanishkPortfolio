## Your Project Notes

Start with Investo, Prepxa, TalentIQ, and Chord Clash. For each, write rough notes answering these prompts:

- What did you personally build? Name the screens, features, code, research, operations, or decisions you owned. Identify teammates and their roles.

- What happened? Give dated user/download/session/revenue figures if available. For a prototype, explain what worked, who tried it, and what remains unfinished. Zero users is fine; don't substitute an estimate.
